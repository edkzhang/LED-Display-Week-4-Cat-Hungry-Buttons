# LED-Display-Week-4-Cat-Hungry-Buttons
When my cat is hungry, they're going to click one of three buttons which will turn the LED on, signifying what type of food they want.

Cat Hungry Buttons PCB ESP32

I began this project by designing the schematic. It was in general pretty easy, but a lot of errors did come up which took a while to solve. The design featured 3 buttons with their own respective LEDS that represent different types of food. A cat would come by and press a button to signify what they feel like eating. It should be easy to train a cat to use. Hopefully they choose something healthy.
<img width="771" height="530" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ecb239d5-186b-4e6a-8b27-6a36d9a31e35" />

After the schematic was done, I worked on the footprints. I assigned different footprints to different features and it was in general pretty straight forward. Hardest part was finding the parts that I would need to use. I then uppdated everything into the PCB editor where I set the layout of the PCB and added the cut out shape accordinly.
<img width="1123" height="607" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/feb14c60-6eab-43fc-a969-f7d2c6cd530c" />

Once the PCB was done, I wired everything together. That was definitely the best part except when I had to restart because I put everything into one layer. Also pretty straightforward to do. Just follow the rat nest lines (blue thin wires).
<img width="1058" height="490" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ae8786b8-96ba-47fc-8068-1a5cf5f947e3" />

Now for the best part of the project, I finally saw my work in 3D using the app's built in 3D viewer. Before I exported the project, I ran a check which resulted in no errors :)
<img width="1059" height="719" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6bd64959-fcda-460d-aa63-fb52c0a33c12" />

Finally, I just loaded everything in and prepared to order. Some modifications I decided on was adding some sort of bigger button on top of the smaller ones I originally had so it would be easier for cats to use.
